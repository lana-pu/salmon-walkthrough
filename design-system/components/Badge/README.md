Small pill that labels or reports status.

**Use** `<span class="badge">`: radius `radius-pill`, padding 4 × 8, `text-caption` in `color-text-muted` on `color-background-muted`, optional 12 icon or a live `.dot`.

**Variants.** `badge on-photo`: `color-glass` fill, `color-text-text`, for status over a photo. `badge offer`: white, 14 semibold, pill padding (`space-pill-pad-y` × `space-pill-pad-x`), for a price or offer over a photo. Position it with `space-12` from the photo corner.

**Points** `badge points`: “+10” in `text-caption`, muted until earned; add `on` for `color-background-interactive` once earned.

**Ink** `badge ink`: `color-background-inverse`, `color-text-on-inverse`, radius `radius-inner`, padding 14 × 18 (`space-14` × `space-18`). For a standing note on a screen (“Estimation only”).
