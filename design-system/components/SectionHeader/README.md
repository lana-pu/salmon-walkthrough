Section title with an optional caption, above a card or a group of rows.

**Use** `<div class="sec-h"><p class="h4 text-section-title">Title</p><p class="c text-caption">Caption</p></div>`. Title `h4` (17 semibold), caption `text-caption` in `color-text-muted`, 8 apart (`space-card-line`). Inset 2 from the card edge (`space-inline-tight`) and `space-page-group` (16) above the content it introduces.
