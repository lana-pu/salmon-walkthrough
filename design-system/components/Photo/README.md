Rounded-square picture of a person, place or product.

**Use** `<img class="photo" src="…">`. Sizes: `photo sm` 56 (`size-photo-sm`), default 72 (`size-photo-md`), `photo lg` 88 (`size-photo-lg`). Radius `radius-inner` (12) at every size; `color-background-muted` shows while the picture loads. The picture is passed in separately, like Avatar.

Round faces are Avatar, not Photo.
