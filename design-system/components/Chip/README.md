Pill filter in a horizontal scroller; the chosen one turns ink.

**Use** `<div class="chips" role="tablist">` with `<button class="chip" role="tab">` items. The scroller bleeds to the screen edges (−24 margin, 24 padding) and hides its scrollbar.

**Sizes.** Default: `size-chip-height` (32), padding `space-pill-pad-x` (12), `text-chip`, white. `chip wcat`: 36 tall with a 16 icon, `text-caption-medium`, 4 apart (places). `chip sm`: 28 tall (`size-chip-height-sm`) with 10 side padding, only for the floor switch on the map. `chip muted`: muted fill instead of white, only on a white ground (amenities on a white card). On the grey page chips stay white (reward filters on m8).

**On:** `color-background-interactive` with `color-text-on-inverse`.
