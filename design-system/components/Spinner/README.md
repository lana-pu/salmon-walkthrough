Ring that turns while something loads.

**Use** `<i class="spinner"></i>` on light surfaces: 16 (`size-icon-md`), 2 ring (`stroke-control`) in `color-border-border` with a `color-border-strong` top. `spinner on-dark` on ink: paper at 35% with a paper top (busy button). Turns once every .8s; still under reduced motion.
