The Salmon icon set: round line icons on a 24 grid with a 1.5 line and round ends. Most come from Misha Naer’s round line catalog, with their outlines thinned from 2 to 1.5; camera, gift, gem, store, truck, bowl, baby, sofa, stairs the single sparkle, and bolt, timer15, timer30, hourglass, sunset, car and passport (option lists: time and ID type) are Salmon’s own, drawn with a 1.5 stroke. Dense icons (mail, store, gift, ID card, receipt, wallet, bank, image) are drawn 5% smaller and the gem 7% smaller, so every icon carries the same visual weight.

The files are drawn in `color-ink` (#1b1a1b). In the product they take a colour token: `color-icon` when the icon acts or stands alone, `color-icon-muted` inside holders and on sheet close, `color-icon-soft` for chevrons, `color-icon-on-inverse` on ink.

Four sizes: `size-icon-xs` 12 in small pills, `size-icon-sm` 14 in 24 holders and controls, `size-icon-md` 16 next to text, `size-icon-topbar` 20 on its own.
