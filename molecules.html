Molecules

Molecules are made of atoms. They are grouped by how they are built: one structure is one molecule, with variants. Different structures are joined only when one turns into the other, like a chip that becomes a card.

Matrices are drawn live with the prototype’s styles. Where there is no matrix, one picture per variant from the screens is shown on its own: the red dashed line is its edge, the checkerboard is where it has no fill.

Organisms, for later

Bigger parts

These hold molecules and come after them: top bar, footer, sheet, cards with rows, ticket, pickup card (its “Ready now” pill becomes a Badge with an icon), invite card with QR, points card, reward card, locked reward, help card, the “You’re here” sheet.

Text styles (notes under footers, notices, centred lines) are typography from the foundations, not molecules. The push notification and the status bar imitate iOS, so they follow the system look, not Salmon tokens.
